Straight answers.

Will my customers know it isn't me?
Shug answers as your business and handles the call like a front desk would. It is not pretending to be you personally. If the caller needs you specifically, Shug can transfer them.
What happens after 120 minutes?
120 minutes a month is included in the $99. That covers normal call volume for most one-truck and two-truck operations. If you consistently run past it, we will talk about it before you get a surprise — not after.
Can it actually book a job on my calendar?
Yes. Shug can book an appointment or create the task on your side so the job lands where you already work, instead of sitting in a voicemail box you check at nine at night.
What does the $199 setup cover?
Setting Shug up for your business specifically — your services, your service area, how you want calls handled, and what counts as urgent enough to reach you. That is the work that makes it sound like your company instead of a generic answering service.
