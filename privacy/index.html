Privacy policy

Last updated August 27, 2026

Short version: this site collects your name and phone number when you hand them over, so I can call you back. It does not sell anything to anyone. Below is the specific version.

What this site collects

What you type into a form. Name, phone number, and trade. Nothing else is requested and nothing else is required. The page you submitted from is recorded alongside it so I know which page brought you in.

What your browser stores locally. If you submit a form, this site saves a flag in your browser's local storage so it shows you the thank-you message instead of an empty form when you come back. That data stays on your device. It is not transmitted anywhere and I cannot read it.

Aggregate traffic data, if analytics are enabled. If Cloudflare Web Analytics is running on this site, it records page views and referrers without cookies and without tracking individuals across sites. It does not build a profile of you.

This site does not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, session recording, or third-party marketing tags.

Who processes it

Formspree receives and forwards form submissions. Their privacy policy governs how they handle it in transit.

Stripe handles payment if you subscribe. Payment details go directly to Stripe and are never seen or stored by Shug or by this website.

Cloudflare serves analytics, if enabled.

No one else. Your information is not sold, rented, traded, or shared with advertisers or data brokers. That is not a policy position I expect praise for — it should be the floor.

Why it is collected

To call you back about the thing you asked about. That is the entire purpose.

If you become a client, your contact details are also used to deliver the service you are paying for and to reach you about your account.

How long it is kept

Lead submissions are kept while there is a reasonable chance of doing business, and deleted on request at any point. Client records are kept for as long as the account is active plus whatever period tax and accounting rules require.

Your choices

You can ask what is held about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Email trent@joinshug.com and it gets handled. There is no form and no verification runaround.

You can also clear your browser's local storage at any time, which removes the submission flag this site stored on your device.

Texts and calls

Giving your phone number here means you are asking to be called about your inquiry. If you become a client, the automations we set up may send texts on your behalf to your customers — that relationship and its consent obligations are covered in the terms, and complying with messaging law for your own customer list is your responsibility as the sender.

Shug will not add you to a marketing text list from a form submission on this site.

Children

This site is for business owners and is not directed at anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top changes with it.

Contact

Questions about any of this: trent@joinshug.com.
