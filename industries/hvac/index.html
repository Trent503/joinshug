The seasonal problem nobody solves in season

HVAC runs on a curve nothing else in the trades does. The first hot week and the first hard freeze produce more demand than you can serve. Then there are months where the phone barely moves.

The trap is that you only think about marketing during the peak, which is exactly when it is too late to do anything about it. Ranking takes months. The company that owns the first hot week did that work in March.

The second trap is that peak weeks are not actually your problem. You are already turning work away. The problem is the shoulder months — and the fact that during the peak you are too busy to capture the replacement leads that are worth the most.

Where HVAC companies leak jobs

Replacement traffic gets treated like repair traffic. Someone searching whether to repair or replace a fifteen-year-old system is a $9,000 decision walking around loose. If your site has nothing that speaks to that decision, they will find a company that does, and that company gets the install.

Maintenance plans are not sold anywhere. Maintenance agreements are the closest thing to recurring revenue in this trade. They smooth the shoulder months and they hand you the replacement job when the system finally dies. Most HVAC sites mention them in a footer.

Peak-week calls go unanswered. The week it hits 98 degrees, everyone is in the field and the phone is ringing constantly. The overflow is not lost demand — it is lost revenue that a text-back would have held onto.

The customer list goes cold between seasons. You installed their system four years ago. They have not heard from you since. When it fails they Google it like they never met you.

Straight answers

Questions HVAC owners ask.

When should I start if I want to own next summer?

Three to six months ahead. The site is live in 24 hours, but organic position takes a season to settle. Starting in the shoulder months is the whole strategy.

Can you help me sell more maintenance plans?

Yes, and it is usually the highest-leverage thing on an HVAC site. A real page explaining what the plan covers and what it saves, plus the re-engagement automation reminding past customers it exists.

Do you do financing integrations?

We surface your financing offer prominently and link to whatever provider you already use. We do not set up the financing relationship itself.

My schedule is already full in July. Why would I market?

Because July is not the problem. The shoulder months are, and the replacement jobs you are too busy to chase in July are worth more than the repairs you are doing. Marketing is what evens the curve out.

What if I only do residential?

That is most of who we build for. If you do a commercial mix, say so on the call and the site gets built to reflect it rather than pretending you only do one.
