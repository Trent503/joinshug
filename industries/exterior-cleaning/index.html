Where exterior cleaning leaks money

The before-and-afters are on your phone, not your site. This trade has the best visual proof of any trade there is. The transformation is dramatic, instant, and photographs beautifully. And most operators have hundreds of these sitting in a camera roll while their website shows a stock photo of a wand.

Pricing is a mystery, so people do not call. Homeowners have no reference point for what a house wash costs. If your site says nothing, a chunk of them assume it is expensive and never inquire. Publishing a starting range filters out the bargain hunters and raises the quality of the calls you do get.

Every job is treated as a one-off. Houses get dirty on a schedule. A customer you washed last April needs it again this April. If nobody reminds them, they either forget or call whoever is advertising. Rebooking your existing list is the cheapest revenue in this business and almost nobody systematizes it.

Route density is ignored. Three jobs in one neighborhood is a completely different day than three jobs across the county. Marketing that pulls from everywhere at once quietly destroys your margin through drive time.

Quotes go unanswered. Someone fills out your form on a Saturday. You see it Monday. They booked someone Sunday.

Straight answers

Questions exterior cleaning owners ask.

Should I really publish my prices?

A starting range, yes. Not a full price list. It filters out people shopping for the cheapest wand in the county and it raises the quality of every call you take. The operators who hide pricing entirely get more inquiries and close fewer of them.

I am a one-truck operation. Is this overkill?

No, it is the opposite. A one-truck operator has the least time to chase leads manually, which makes the automations worth more to you than to a company with an office manager.

How do I get more work in one neighborhood?

Service-area pages built for the neighborhoods you want, plus re-engagement timed so your existing customers in that area rebook in the same window. Density is built, not lucky.

Does soft washing need its own page?

If you sell it as a distinct service, yes. Roof cleaning especially — homeowners search for it specifically and it carries a much higher ticket than a driveway.

Will this work if I am seasonal and shut down in winter?

Yes. We set hours and messaging honestly, keep the site working through the off season so you hold your rankings, and time the re-engagement push to your season opening.
