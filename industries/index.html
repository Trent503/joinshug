Straight answers

Questions about trade fit

Do you only work with these six trades?

No. These are the trades with their own page because their call patterns differ most. Painting, window cleaning, gutter cleaning, junk removal, pest control, tree service, concrete, remodeling, general contracting and cleaning companies all work the same way — Shug is configured around your services and your call rules during setup regardless of trade.

Why does the trade change how the AI receptionist works?

Because the questions that matter are different. A roofing call needs the address and whether water is coming in. An HVAC call needs to know whether anyone in the house is vulnerable. A landscaping call needs to establish whether it is recurring work or a one-off install. Setup is where those questions get defined for your trade.
