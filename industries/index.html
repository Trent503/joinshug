Industries

Built for the trade, not for “home services.”

A roofing site and a plumbing site are not the same page with different photos. Different intent, different ticket size, different seasons, different questions. Pick your trade.

Roofing

Storm surges, insurance-claim traffic, and a ticket big enough that every homeowner gets three quotes.

Plumbing

Emergency intent, where the first company to answer the phone wins the job and nobody leaves a voicemail.

HVAC

Brutal seasonal peaks, maintenance plans that carry the shoulder months, and replace-or-repair traffic.

Exterior cleaning

Visual proof, route density, and the annual cycle. The trade I actually ran to $243K a year.

Landscaping

Recurring maintenance contracts over one-off mows, plus design-build work worth ten mowing accounts.

Why the trade changes the build

Emergency trades win on speed of response. A burst pipe does not shop around, so the whole build points at answering fast and catching what you miss.

High-ticket trades win on surviving comparison. Nobody gets three quotes for a drain clog and everybody gets three for a roof, so the site has to hold up against two competitors open in other tabs.

Recurring trades win on route density and rebooking. Six lawns on one street is a profitable morning and six across the county is a loss wearing a full schedule.

Seasonal trades win on timing. The company that owns the first hot week did that work months earlier, in the shoulder season when nothing felt urgent.
