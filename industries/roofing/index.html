Roofing does not sell like other trades

Two things make roofing different, and most marketing built for home services ignores both.

The ticket is big enough to shop. Nobody gets three quotes for a clogged drain. Everybody gets three quotes for a $12,000 roof. That means you are not competing to be found — you are competing to be the one they trust after they have already found three of you. Your site is doing a different job than a plumber's site. It has to survive comparison.

Demand arrives in spikes. A hailstorm rolls through and search volume for roof repair in your area goes up several hundred percent in a day. Then it decays over about three weeks. Whoever is visible during that window takes the season. Whoever is not waits for the next storm.

Those two facts should drive everything, and for most roofing companies they drive nothing at all.

Where roofing companies leak jobs

The site does not survive the comparison. A homeowner with three tabs open is looking for a reason to eliminate one. Stock photos of a roof that is not yours, no license number, no real gallery, a form that wants their address before it will talk to them. Any of those closes the tab.

Nothing addresses the insurance question. A huge share of roofing searches after a storm are really insurance questions in disguise. Do I have a claim. Will my rate go up. Who talks to the adjuster. If your site does not answer those plainly, you look like a company that does not handle claims, and the one down the street that explains the process gets the call.

The storm window closes before you rank. You cannot start SEO the week the hail hits. The companies that own storm traffic built their position months earlier.

Inspection requests go to voicemail. Everyone is on a roof. That is the job. Which means roofing has one of the worst missed-call problems of any trade, on the exact days when the leads are worth the most.

Straight answers

Questions roofers ask.

Do you handle insurance claim marketing?

We build the pages and the copy that answer homeowner insurance questions clearly, which is what wins that traffic. We do not advise on claims themselves and we will not write anything that promises a claim outcome. That is your expertise and your liability, not ours.

How fast can I be live before storm season?

The site is live in 24 hours. SEO position takes longer — figure three to six months for organic to settle, which is why the right time to start is well before the season you want to own.

Is Local Services Ads worth it for roofing?

Usually yes, and roofing is the clearest case of any trade. Run your own numbers: average job value times your inbound close rate is what a lead is worth. For most roofers that number is high enough that per-lead pricing is comfortable.

Can you market storm chasing?

No. We build for companies that work a market they actually operate in, with a real local address and a real license. Out-of-state storm chasing is a different business and it is not one we take on.

Will you build me pages for cities I do not serve?

No. Inventing service areas is how you get filtered out of local results and it puts your Google Business Profile at risk. We build pages for where you genuinely work.
