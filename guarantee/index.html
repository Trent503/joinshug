The Shug guarantee

If Shug doesn't pay for itself, you don't pay for Shug.

Thirty days. Full refund. No forms, no fight. Here is exactly what that means and what it covers.

THE SHUG GUARANTEE

Three promises, in plain English.

30-day money back

Full refund in the first month. No questions asked.

No contracts

Month to month. Cancel with one text.

You own it all

Your site, your domain, your customer list — handed over if you ever leave.

Why this exists

Almost every operator I talk to has been burned by an agency. Same story most times: a twelve-month contract, a slow start, a promise that it takes time to work, and month nine arriving with nothing to show and no way out.

So the guarantee is not a marketing flourish. It is the direct answer to the actual reason people hesitate. If you can leave whenever you want, and get your first month back, then the risk of trying this is close to zero — and the pressure is entirely on me to make it worth keeping.

That is the arrangement I would have wanted when I was running my own trade business. Nobody offered it. So it is the one I built.

The 30-day money-back guarantee

Thirty days from your first payment, you can ask for a full refund of what you have paid Shug. You text me and it goes back to your card.

There is no form to fill out, no retention call, and no requirement to explain yourself. I would rather refund someone cleanly than keep money from a person who does not want to be here.

No contracts, ever

Everything is month to month. There is no minimum term, no notice period, and no cancellation fee. If you want to stop in month four, you stop in month four.

Practically that means the only thing keeping you here is whether the work is producing. Which is how it should be, and it is why the pricing has no setup fee attached — I do not need to front-load revenue from people who are about to leave.

You own everything

This is the part that gets people, because it is the part agencies usually get wrong.

The website is yours. Not licensed to you, not rented. If you leave, you get the files and we help you move them.

The domain is yours. Registered to you, in your name. This is worth checking with whoever you use now — a surprising number of agencies register the domain to themselves.

The customer list is yours. Every lead the site captures, exported and handed over.

The ad account is yours. On The Monopoly, the Google Ads and Local Services Ads accounts are set up under your business with your billing. The history and the data stay with you.

What the guarantee does not cover

Being straight about the edges of it.

Ad spend is not refundable, because it goes to Google and is consumed when the ads run. The guarantee covers what you pay Shug.

It is also not a guarantee of a specific ranking, a specific number of leads, or a specific revenue figure. Anyone promising you page one by a certain date is either lying or targeting keywords nobody searches. What is guaranteed is that you can leave with your money back if you do not think it is worth it — which is the only promise I can make honestly.

What I ask in return

Send the photos. Answer the intake call. Tell me when something is wrong instead of going quiet.

That is the whole list. The work goes badly when an operator disappears for six weeks and then decides it did not work. It goes well when you treat it like a partner you hired rather than a vendor you are waiting on.

Straight answers

Questions about the guarantee.

How do I claim the refund?

Text me. That is the whole process. There is no form, no exit interview, and no attempt to talk you out of it. The refund goes back to the card you paid with.

How long do I have?

Thirty days from your first payment. If you are past thirty days you can still cancel any time — it is month to month — you just will not get the previous month back.

Do I have to give a reason?

No. If you want to tell me what did not work I will listen, because it makes the next build better. But it is not a condition of anything.

What do I keep if I leave?

The website, the domain, and your customer list. We transfer them to you. Nothing about your web presence stays hostage to a subscription.

Is the guarantee on the ad spend too?

No. Ad spend goes directly to Google and is consumed the moment the ads run, so it is not ours to refund. The guarantee covers what you pay Shug.

Drop your name and number — I'll personally call you within 24 hours. No card, no pressure.
