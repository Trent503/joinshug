This is not that. These are three specific leaks, plugged. You do not log into anything. You do not configure anything. The only thing that changes on your end is that more jobs show up.

Missed-call text back

You are up a ladder, under a sink, or in a crawlspace. The phone rings. You cannot get it.

The homeowner does not leave a voicemail. Almost nobody does anymore. They hang up and call the next result. That job is gone and you never even knew it existed.

The fix is one text, sent automatically, within seconds of the missed call: a note that you are on a job, and a question about what they need. It works because it arrives while they still have the phone in their hand and before they have dialed the next number. The whole window is about two minutes wide and this lands inside it.

Then it is a normal text thread with you. Most people would rather text than talk anyway, and a text thread means you can answer between jobs instead of stopping work.

Worth being specific about the size of this leak. If you miss four calls a week and half of them were real opportunities, that is two a week. Close 80 percent of those at a $400 ticket and it is $33,280 a year walking out the door. The full arithmetic is here.

Lead text notifications

Every form submission on your site texts you immediately with the name, the number, and what they asked for. Not an email you find on Sunday. A text, now.

Speed to lead is the least glamorous competitive advantage in the trades and one of the most reliable. The operator who calls back in five minutes beats the one who calls back in five hours almost every time, regardless of who is better at the actual work.

Review requests, on autopilot

You know you should be asking for reviews. You are not going to sit down at night and do it manually, and neither is anyone else.

So it fires off job completion. Connected to Jobber or Housecall Pro, the request goes out when you close the job — which is the moment the customer is happiest and most likely to act. One text, a direct link to your Google profile, and nothing further if they do not respond.

We do not gate reviews. Filtering for happy customers first is against Google's policies and it is the kind of shortcut that gets a profile's reviews removed wholesale. One honest ask, to everyone.

This compounds into local SEO directly. Review count and recency both feed map pack rankings, and they lower your Local Services Ads cost per lead. The automation that makes your reputation visible also makes your marketing cheaper. More on the review argument here.

Past-customer re-engagement

Your customer list is the most underused asset in your business. Someone who already paid you once, liked the work, and had no complaints is dramatically easier to sell than a stranger who found you on Google.

They just forget. Not because they were unhappy — because a year passed and nobody reminded them.

So we set up seasonal re-engagement against your list. The timing depends on the trade: HVAC before the first cold snap and the first hot week, exterior cleaning on an annual cycle from the last job, landscaping at season open, roofing after a storm event in your area.

It is the cheapest revenue in the business and it costs you nothing but the list you already have.

Straight answers

Questions operators ask about the automations.

Is this software I have to learn?

No. That is the point. We set it up, connect it to your number and your job software, and it runs. There is no dashboard you are required to log into and no new app for your crew. If you can read a text message you can use it.

What happens when a customer replies to the automatic text?

It goes to you like a normal text conversation. The automation sends the first message so the lead does not go cold. After that it is you and the customer, the way it should be.

Will the review requests annoy my customers?

One text after the job is done, and it stops if they do not respond. No repeated nagging. We also never gate reviews — asking only happy customers to leave a review violates Google's policies and can get your reviews wiped.

Does it work with Jobber and Housecall Pro?

Yes. Those are the two we connect to most often, and the review request fires off job completion so the timing is right. If you run something else, tell me on the call and I will tell you honestly whether we can hook into it.

Why is this included instead of an add-on?

Because selling it separately would mean pretending it is optional. Missed calls and missing reviews are the two biggest leaks in a trade business. Charging extra to plug them would be charging extra to make the rest of the work function.
