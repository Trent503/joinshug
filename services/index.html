The order matters

These are not four things you pick from a menu. They stack, and doing them out of order wastes money.

The site comes first, because SEO has nothing to rank without pages worth ranking and ads burn money pointing at a page that does not convert. Then local SEO, because it compounds — it takes three to six months to settle and then keeps paying. Then ads, but only if your average job value and close rate justify per-lead pricing.

The automations are included at every tier rather than sold as an upgrade, because they are not optional. A site that generates calls you then drop is not a solution to anything.
