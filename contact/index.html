Contact

Drop your number. I'll call you.

The blueprint call is 15 minutes, free, and it's me on the phone — not a rep working from a script. I'll call within 24 hours.

What the call actually is

Fifteen minutes. No slides, no proposal document, no follow-up sequence.

I ask what trade you're in, what your service area looks like, what your average job is worth, and where the work comes from now. Then I tell you what I'd do about it — including whether that's anything at all.

If your numbers do not support paid ads, I will say so instead of selling you the top tier. If your site is fine and the real problem is that you never ask for reviews, I will tell you that too, and it is a much cheaper fix.

What it is not

It is not a discovery call that turns into a second discovery call. It is not a pitch with a countdown timer on it. And it does not need a credit card, because there is nothing to buy on the call.

What to have ready

Nothing, really. But the call is more useful if you know roughly what your average job is worth and roughly what portion of inbound leads you close. Those two numbers decide most of what I would recommend.

Prefer to just start?

You do not have to talk to me first. Pick a tier on the pricing page and start — there is no contract, and the 30-day money-back guarantee means the risk of doing it that way is the same as the risk of doing it after a call.
