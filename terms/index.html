Terms of service

Last updated August 27, 2026

These terms cover using this website and subscribing to Shug. They are written to be read rather than to be impenetrable.

The service

Shug provides website design and hosting, local SEO, managed advertising, and follow-up automations for trade businesses, at the tiers described on the pricing page. What is included at each tier is what is listed there.

Billing

Subscriptions are monthly, billed in advance through Stripe, and renew automatically until cancelled. Prices are in US dollars.

There is no setup fee, no build fee, and no minimum term. Advertising spend on The Monopoly tier is billed separately and directly by Google to your own account — it is not part of the Shug subscription.

Cancelling

Cancel at any time by texting or emailing. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. There is no cancellation fee and no notice requirement.

Refunds

A full refund of fees paid to Shug is available within 30 days of your first payment. Details are on the guarantee page. Advertising spend paid to Google is not refundable by Shug, because it is consumed by Google when the ads run.

What you own

On cancellation you keep the website, the domain, your customer and lead data, and any advertising accounts set up in your name. These are transferred to you on request.

Shug retains ownership of its own underlying tooling, templates, and processes. You own your instance and your content, not the toolkit it was built with.

What you're responsible for

The accuracy of what you tell us to publish — your licenses, insurance, certifications, service areas, guarantees, and pricing. We will not publish claims we cannot verify, and we are not liable for claims you supply that turn out to be inaccurate.

Any content you provide, including photographs, must be yours to use.

Compliance with messaging and calling law for your own customer list. The automations send on your behalf, which makes you the sender. Consent, opt-outs, and quiet hours are your obligation, and we will set the systems up to support them.

Answering your phone. It sounds flippant. It is the single biggest determinant of whether any of this works.

What we don't promise

No specific ranking, lead volume, revenue figure, or return on spend is guaranteed. Search and advertising platforms are controlled by third parties and change without notice.

What is guaranteed is the 30-day refund, which is the only promise that can be made honestly.

Acceptable use

We will not build or market fabricated reviews, fake service locations, unverifiable certifications, or claims about licensing or insurance that are not true. This is not squeamishness — it is how businesses get manual actions from Google and removed from local results entirely.

We reserve the right to end an engagement, with a prorated refund, where continuing would require any of the above.

Liability

Shug's total liability for any claim relating to the service is limited to the fees you paid in the three months before the claim arose. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential losses.

Changes

These terms may change. Material changes will be communicated to active clients before they take effect, and the date at the top will change.
